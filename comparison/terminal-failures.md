# Terminal failures

## Work after a job fails for good

An implementation given `onFailure` to `task.implement` handles the jobs that fail for good: the transaction that fails such a job, after a `TerminalError` or on its last attempt, also adds a pending job that runs `onFailure`, the failure job, whose payload records the id, the reason, the error message, and the time of the failure, the metadata of the job, and its payload.
The failure job runs and retries as any job, of a task named after the task of the failed job, with its own attempts, lease, and backoff, and fails for good itself once they run out.

- pg-boss, same: the statement that fails a job after all its retries also inserts a copy of its payload into the `deadLetter` queue of its queue, with the name, id, retry count, and output of the failed job in `source_*` columns ([docs](https://github.com/timgit/pg-boss/blob/12.38.1/docs/api/queues.md?plain=1#L69-L71), [source](https://github.com/timgit/pg-boss/blob/12.38.1/src/plans.ts#L3532-L3562)); the copy runs under the retry, expiration, and heartbeat options of the dead letter queue.
- BullMQ, differs: a `Worker` emits its local `failed` event after it records each failed attempt ([source](https://github.com/taskforcesh/bullmq/blob/v6.7.5/src/classes/worker.ts#L1203-L1236)); the script that fails a job for good appends a `failed` entry to the event stream of the queue, and a `retries-exhausted` entry only when the job has used all its attempts ([source](https://github.com/taskforcesh/bullmq/blob/v6.7.5/src/commands/moveToFinished-14.lua#L198-L205)), which keeps about 10,000 entries by default ([source](https://github.com/taskforcesh/bullmq/blob/v6.7.5/src/commands/includes/trimEvents.lua#L8-L15)) and which `QueueEvents` reads with `XREAD` from its newest entry unless given `lastEventId`, with no acknowledgment ([source](https://github.com/taskforcesh/bullmq/blob/v6.7.5/src/classes/queue-events.ts#L371-L388), [source](https://github.com/taskforcesh/bullmq/blob/v6.7.5/src/classes/redis-queue-backend.ts#L2926-L2931)); the same script moves the parent of a child added to a flow with `continueParentOnFailure`, a job created with the flow, to wait ([docs](https://github.com/taskforcesh/bullmq/blob/v6.7.5/docs/gitbook/guide/flows/continue-parent.md?plain=1#L13-L18), [source](https://github.com/taskforcesh/bullmq/blob/v6.7.5/src/commands/includes/moveChildFromDependenciesIfNeeded.lua#L53-L58)).
- Graphile Worker, differs: a worker emits the local event `job:failed` for a job that failed on its last attempt before it records the failure ([source](https://github.com/graphile/worker/blob/v0.18.0/src/worker.ts#L295-L356)).
- Sidekiq, differs: the `sidekiq_retries_exhausted` block of a job class runs in the process once the last retry fails, before the job moves to the dead set, and the global death handlers after it; an exception from the block is reported through the error handlers of Sidekiq, and the processing of the death goes on ([source](https://github.com/sidekiq/sidekiq/blob/v8.1.7/lib/sidekiq/job_retry.rb#L250-L281)).
- Inngest, differs: `onFailure` of a function registers a separate function that the event `inngest/function.failed` of the function triggers once it runs out of retries, and its step is registered with `retries: { attempts: 1 }`, the setting that the `retries` option of an ordinary function sets, whatever the `retries` of the function ([source](https://github.com/inngest/inngest-js/blob/inngest%404.26.2/packages/inngest/src/components/InngestFunction.ts#L226-L253), [source](https://github.com/inngest/inngest-js/blob/inngest%404.26.2/packages/inngest/src/components/InngestFunction.ts#L163-L184)).
- GoodJob, differs: it runs jobs through Active Job of Rails, whose `after_discard` blocks run in the process when a `retry_on` runs out of attempts, at a `discard_on`, and at any exception that no handler rescues, before the exception reaches GoodJob ([Rails 8.1.2 source](https://github.com/rails/rails/blob/v8.1.2/activejob/lib/active_job/exceptions.rb#L64-L133), [Rails 8.1.2 source](https://github.com/rails/rails/blob/v8.1.2/activejob/lib/active_job/execution.rb#L45-L58)); the `on_discard` callback job of a batch is enqueued at the first discarded job of the batch ([docs](https://github.com/bensheldon/good_job/blob/v4.24.1/README.md?plain=1#L947-L953)) by the process that ran the job, after it releases its claim ([source](https://github.com/bensheldon/good_job/blob/v4.24.1/app/models/good_job/job/lockable.rb#L97-L112), [source](https://github.com/bensheldon/good_job/blob/v4.24.1/app/models/good_job/batch_record.rb#L58-L75)).
- Solid Queue, differs: it runs jobs through Active Job of Rails, whose `after_discard` blocks run in the process as for GoodJob, and records a job whose exception reaches it as failed, with no retry of its own ([docs](https://github.com/rails/solid_queue/blob/v1.7.2/README.md?plain=1#L650-L658)); the `on_failure` callback job of a batch is enqueued once all jobs of the batch have finished and one of them has failed ([docs](https://github.com/rails/solid_queue/blob/v1.7.2/README.md?plain=1#L710-L722)), in a transaction after the one that records the last job, which a sweep of the dispatcher repeats if it fails ([source](https://github.com/rails/solid_queue/blob/v1.7.2/app/models/solid_queue/batch_execution.rb#L12-L53), [docs](https://github.com/rails/solid_queue/blob/v1.7.2/README.md?plain=1#L782-L784)).
- Que, differs: `handle_error` of a job class, which a job class may override, runs in the worker after the job raises, and by default expires the job once its error count exceeds `maximum_retry_count` ([source](https://github.com/que-rb/que/blob/v2.4.1/lib/que/job_methods.rb#L42-L70), [source](https://github.com/que-rb/que/blob/v2.4.1/lib/que/job_methods.rb#L127-L138)).
- Oban, differs: once it has discarded a job that failed on its last attempt, the process that ran the job emits the telemetry event `[:oban, :job, :exception]` with the state `:discard` ([source](https://github.com/oban-bg/oban/blob/v2.24.2/lib/oban/queues/executor.ex#L220-L288)).

## A job that fails for good by recovery

A job that fails for good without its handler — its lease expires on its last attempt, such as after its worker crashed, a worker that starts with the id of its worker returns it on its last attempt, or it is taken too late to start — gets its failure job from the same statement of the reaper or of the worker that fails it, which runs no code of its task.

- pg-boss, same: the sweeps that fail the jobs whose expiration or heartbeat passed use the same statement as a failure of a handler, which inserts the dead letter copy of a job that has no retries left ([source](https://github.com/timgit/pg-boss/blob/12.38.1/src/plans.ts#L3268-L3296)).
- BullMQ, differs: the stalled-job check moves a job that has stalled more than `maxStalledCount` times, and that it does not recognise as repeatable, back to wait with a deferred failure ([source](https://github.com/taskforcesh/bullmq/blob/v6.7.5/src/commands/moveStalledJobsToWait-9.lua#L76-L105)), and the worker that takes it next fails it without running its processor ([source](https://github.com/taskforcesh/bullmq/blob/v6.7.5/src/classes/worker.ts#L1072-L1094), [source](https://github.com/taskforcesh/bullmq/blob/v6.7.5/src/classes/worker.ts#L1157-L1162)), through the script and the events of any failure for good.
- Graphile Worker, lacks: a worker counts an attempt when it takes a job ([source](https://github.com/graphile/worker/blob/v0.18.0/src/sql/getJobs.ts#L246)), and a job whose attempts reached `max_attempts` is no longer available ([source](https://github.com/graphile/worker/blob/v0.18.0/sql/000011.sql#L67)), so a job whose worker died on its last attempt, once unlocked after 4 hours ([source](https://github.com/graphile/worker/blob/v0.18.0/src/sql/resetLockedAt.ts#L18-L28)), stays in the table with no `job:failed` event and no error of that attempt.
- Sidekiq, lacks: its open-source fetch takes a job off Redis, so a job that runs when its process dies neither returns nor reaches the retry handler ([source](https://github.com/sidekiq/sidekiq/blob/v8.1.7/lib/sidekiq/fetch.rb#L48-L72)).
- GoodJob, differs: a job of a process that died runs again, and with the extension `GoodJob::ActiveJobExtensions::InterruptErrors` it raises `GoodJob::InterruptError` in the process that takes it, so that `retry_on` and `discard_on` decide ([docs](https://github.com/bensheldon/good_job/blob/v4.24.1/README.md?plain=1#L1230-L1244)).
- Solid Queue, differs: another process that prunes a dead process, or the supervisor that reaps a killed fork, records its claimed jobs as failed without running their code, so Active Job runs no `after_discard` and no `retry_on` for them, and emits the notification `fail_many_claimed.solid_queue` with their ids in that process ([docs](https://github.com/rails/solid_queue/blob/v1.7.2/README.md?plain=1#L687-L708), [source](https://github.com/rails/solid_queue/blob/v1.7.2/app/models/solid_queue/claimed_execution.rb#L51-L66)); such a failure counts toward the `on_failure` callback of a batch ([source](https://github.com/rails/solid_queue/blob/v1.7.2/app/models/solid_queue/job/retryable.rb#L18-L21), [source](https://github.com/rails/solid_queue/blob/v1.7.2/app/models/solid_queue/failed_execution/batchable.rb#L10-L16)).
- Que, differs: the statements that count an error run only in the worker after the job raised ([source](https://github.com/que-rb/que/blob/v2.4.1/lib/que/job_methods.rb#L11-L33)), so a job of a dead worker runs again with the same error count and does not fail for good by recovery.
- Oban, differs: `Lifeline`, on the leader node, discards a job that has been executing for longer than `rescue_after` once its attempts are exhausted, and reports it only in `discarded_jobs` of its plugin event `[:oban, :plugin, :stop]`, without `[:oban, :job, :exception]` ([source](https://github.com/oban-bg/oban/blob/v2.24.2/lib/oban/lifeline.ex#L42-L54), [source](https://github.com/oban-bg/oban/blob/v2.24.2/lib/oban/lifeline.ex#L170-L184)).

## A job interrupted by stop on its last attempt

A handler that throws after `stop` aborts it, other than a `TerminalError` or a `RetryError`, returns its job to pending also on its last attempt, so the job gets no failure job.

- pg-boss, differs: at its `timeout`, `stop` fails the jobs of the handlers that still run through the statement of any failure, which dead-letters a job that has no retries left ([source](https://github.com/timgit/pg-boss/blob/12.38.1/src/manager.ts#L1298-L1313), [source](https://github.com/timgit/pg-boss/blob/12.38.1/src/plans.ts#L3246-L3251)).
- Graphile Worker, differs: a job whose handler throws after the abort of a shutdown fails like any other ([docs](https://github.com/graphile/worker/blob/v0.18.0/website/docs/error-handling.md#termination-signal)), with the event `job:failed` on its last attempt ([source](https://github.com/graphile/worker/blob/v0.18.0/src/worker.ts#L310-L326)).
- Sidekiq, same: an error caused by the shutdown bypasses the retry handler ([source](https://github.com/sidekiq/sidekiq/blob/v8.1.7/lib/sidekiq/job_retry.rb#L117-L127)), and the jobs still running at the hard shutdown return to their queues ([source](https://github.com/sidekiq/sidekiq/blob/v8.1.7/lib/sidekiq/fetch.rb#L52-L72)).
- GoodJob, same: a job that a shutdown interrupts runs again, whatever `retry_on_unhandled_error` ([docs](https://github.com/bensheldon/good_job/blob/v4.24.1/README.md?plain=1#L327), [docs](https://github.com/bensheldon/good_job/blob/v4.24.1/README.md?plain=1#L1230)).
- Solid Queue, same: the jobs that a worker still runs when it exits at a termination signal return to their queues as the worker deregisters ([docs](https://github.com/rails/solid_queue/blob/v1.7.2/README.md?plain=1#L398-L402)).
